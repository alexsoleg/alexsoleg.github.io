I am a **PhD candidate** at the [Image Processing Group](https://imatge.upc.edu/web/) at Universitat Politècnica de Catalunya (UPC), supervised by Javier Ruiz-Hidalgo and Eliseo Ruiz. I develop graph neural networks and self-supervised learning methods that encode geometry, chemistry, and periodic structure directly.

My research spans atomic representations, crystal property prediction, thermal motion, and multiscale machine-learning force fields. Before the PhD, I worked on computer vision and remote sensing at Vicomtech, TU Delft, and Istituto Italiano di Tecnologia.
