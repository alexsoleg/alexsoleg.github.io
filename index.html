Àlex Solé

PhD candidate in machine learning at Universitat Politècnica de Catalunya, working on graph learning for molecular and crystalline systems.

I am a PhD candidate at the Image Processing Group at Universitat Politècnica de Catalunya (UPC), supervised by Javier Ruiz-Hidalgo and Eliseo Ruiz. My work focuses on graph neural networks and self-supervised learning for molecular and crystalline systems.

My current research asks how structural information can be encoded so that models learn useful representations for materials, molecular qubits, and multiscale physical systems. Recent work includes papers on CartNet for thermal ellipsoid estimation, PRISM for crystal property prediction, MuSE for machine learning multiscale interactions, and on unsupervised atomic embeddings.

Before the PhD, I worked on computer vision and remote sensing at Vicomtech, TU Delft, and Istituto Italiano di Tecnologia. I received my MSc in Telecommunications Engineering and BSc in Telecommunications Technologies and Services Engineering from UPC, with research stays at Télécom ParisTech, TU Delft, and the University of Luxembourg.

Graph learning for materials

Crystal and molecular graphs need geometry, chemistry, and periodic structure. My work builds models that encode these constraints directly.

Molecular and crystalline systems

I work with collaborators in computational chemistry on property prediction, thermal motion, molecular qubits, and force-field learning.

Self-supervised representations

I am interested in pretraining and multiscale representations that use unlabeled structure before labeled data becomes available.

selected publications

  1. arXiv
    Machine Learning Multiscale Interactions
    Àlex Solé, Sergio Suárez-Dou, Albert Mosella-Montoro, Silvia Gómez-Coca, Eliseo Ruiz, and 2 more authors
    arXiv preprint arXiv:2605.25710, 2026
    Abs arXiv Bib HTML

    MuSE is a hierarchical model for machine learning force fields. It pools atomistic systems into coarse nodes, then lets existing force-field modules model interactions across molecular and material length scales.

  2. NPJCM
    PRISM: periodic representation with multiscale and similarity graph modelling for enhanced crystal structure property prediction
    Àlex Solé, Albert Mosella-Montoro, Joan Cardona, Daniel Aravena, Silvia Gómez-Coca, and 2 more authors
    npj Computational Materials, 2026
    Abs DOI arXiv Bib HTML

    PRISM is a graph neural network framework for crystal property prediction. It combines periodic feature encoding with multiscale graph experts so the model can represent local chemistry and longer-range crystal structure in the same prediction pipeline.

  3. DD
    A Cartesian encoding graph neural network for crystal structure property prediction: application to thermal ellipsoid estimation
    Àlex Solé, Albert Mosella-Montoro, Joan Cardona, Silvia Gómez-Coca, Daniel Aravena, and 2 more authors
    Digital Discovery, 2025
    Abs DOI arXiv Bib HTML

    CartNet predicts crystal properties by encoding atomic geometry with respect to Cartesian axes and crystal temperature. The first application estimates anisotropic displacement parameters, which are used to describe thermal ellipsoids in crystal structures.

  4. IJAEOG
    A learning approach for river debris detection
    Àlex Solé, Leonardo Scandolo, and Elmar Eisemann
    International Journal of Applied Earth Observation and Geoinformation, 2022
    Abs DOI Bib HTML

    This work uses Sentinel-2 satellite imagery and segmentation models to detect floating debris in rivers. It includes a dataset of candidate debris regions and compares deep learning approaches for multispectral remote sensing data.
